# MOONLIT FLIGHT
A little journey through a winter night.

Thank you for downloading MoonlitFlight!

HOW TO PLAY

- Press SPACE or the UP ARROW to make the crow fly.
- You can also click to flap your wings.
- Avoid the streetlamps and obstacles.
- Try to survive for as long as you can.
- Press M to mute or unmute the music.

GAME OVER

When your journey ends, take a moment to read the little poem.

Every flight has an ending, but that doesn't make the journey any less beautiful.

HOW TO OPEN THE GAME

1. Extract the MoonlitFlight ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted MoonlitFlight folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. Your browser will open the game!

The game is designed to run locally without an internet connection.

Enjoy your flight. 🌙
